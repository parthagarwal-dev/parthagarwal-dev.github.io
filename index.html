Current Research · UIUC

Verifiable Agentic AI for Scientific Applications

With Prof. Haohan Wang, I am developing a compiler-inspired scientific-agent architecture that converts open-ended scientific intent into typed plans, registry-bound primitives, deterministic execution, and verifier-authorized results.

My work includes reusable scientific capabilities across single-cell, transcriptomics, and genomics, along with verifier-side recomputation, provenance checks, adversarial testing, repeat-parity checks, and fail-closed recovery.

Current manuscript in preparation for submission to RECOMB 2027.

Planned Thesis Research

Reliable Adaptation of Pretrained Models under Structured Distribution Shift

I am interested in determining, without target labels, when adapting a pretrained model to a shifted deployment environment is likely to help or hurt.

The broader goal is to develop reliability signals that can support a selective decision: adapt when evidence suggests adaptation is beneficial, or retain the original model when adaptation is likely to degrade performance.
