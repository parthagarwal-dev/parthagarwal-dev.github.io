Agentic AI · AI for science

May 2026–Present

Verifiable agentic AI for computational biology

Dr. Haohan Wang · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

Manuscript under submission · RECOMB 2027

I am developing a compiler-inspired architecture that turns open-ended scientific requests into typed plans, executes registered scientific capabilities, and independently verifies the resulting computations. The research focuses on making scientific-agent behavior inspectable and results traceable to the data and steps that produced them.

  • My contributions: semantic input resolution, typed execution contracts, reusable scientific capabilities, and verifier-driven recovery.
  • Scientific scope: single-cell, transcriptomic, genomic, and emerging epigenomic workflows.

Multimodal AI · Clinical NLP

Sep 2025–Aug 2026

Multimodal EHR modeling and clinical prediction

Dr. Fenglong Ma · Penn State University

Funded SURE fellowship · Summer 2026

My work with Dr. Ma investigated combining EHR time series and clinical text for prediction when some modalities are unavailable. I worked on modular data pipelines, modality-specific encoders, masked-attention fusion, and evaluation of reliability under missing inputs and distribution shift.

  • Modeling: explicit missing-modality handling, transformer fusion, and single-modality and early/late-fusion comparisons.
  • Analysis: calibration, modality attribution, feature explanations, ablations, and robustness.

Explainable AI · Computer vision

Nov 2024–Aug 2025

Explainable gait abnormality detection

Dr. Suman Saha · Penn State University

Conference paper · ICMLA 2025

I developed CNN-LSTM modeling and training pipelines for gait analysis using GAVD and OU-MVLP. The published work studies joint-based features and silhouette sequences, with SHAP and Grad-CAM for understanding the temporal and spatial information behind predictions.

  • My contributions: PyTorch pipelines, classical and deep-learning baselines, sequence preprocessing, and class-imbalance handling.
  • Evaluation: testing on unseen identities and analysis of interpretable model behavior.
