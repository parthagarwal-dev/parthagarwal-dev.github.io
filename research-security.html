Systems and network security at SyNSec

Recognized research contributor · NSF Award 2215017

Undergraduate research · Dr. Syed Rafiul Hussain
Penn State University · Dec 2023–Aug 2025

Research experience in systematic software testing and a recognized contribution to an NSF-supported systems project.

Research role

I volunteered in Penn State’s Systems and Network Security Lab under Dr. Syed Rafiul Hussain from December 2023 to August 2025. My lab work involved testing and vulnerability analysis for cellular and IoT systems.

Technical contributions

I simulated and ran fuzzing tools including AFL, AFL++, AFLNet, and LibAFL to examine unexpected behavior and potential vulnerabilities. I also developed C++ and Python tooling in virtual-machine environments to support testing and further tool development.

NSF research contribution

I was recognized as a contributor to research supported by NSF Award 2215017: Collaborative Research: CNS Core: Large: Systems and Verifiable Metrics for Sustainable Data Centers.

The funded project develops systems and metrics for more sustainable data-center computing. Dr. Hussain is the Penn State principal investigator. The linked institutional and NSF records describe the funded project.
