Agentic AI · AI for science

Verifiable agentic AI for computational biology

Manuscript under submission · RECOMB 2027

Undergraduate research · Dr. Haohan Wang
University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign · May 2026–Present

How can scientific agents produce results whose computational and scientific validity can be checked independently?

Research problem

A scientific agent must preserve the meaning of its inputs, execute an appropriate analysis, and provide evidence for its conclusions. My work with Dr. Haohan Wang extends the existing HEART framework to make these steps explicit and independently checkable.

My contribution & design rationale

My focus is the connection between scientific intent, reusable analysis capabilities, and verification: defining what an analysis requires, how it runs, and what evidence is needed before releasing its output.

Make input meaning explicit before execution

I work on semantic role resolution and typed execution contracts for matrices, cohorts, conditions, covariates, annotations, and parameters across H5AD, MTX, VCF, and BED inputs. These contracts make the assumptions of a scientific analysis inspectable before a tool runs.

Separate reusable methods from task-specific requests

I extend registered capabilities for pseudobulk differential expression, regulatory-network inference with GENIE3 and GRNBoost2, co-expression analysis with WGCNA, enrichment, and variant or peak annotation. Shared input and output contracts let the agent compose these capabilities across workflows. Current extensions include epigenomic input resolution and motif-related analysis.

Verify the evidence independently

My verification work includes recomputing key quantities, checking provenance and hashes, testing adversarial inputs, and comparing repeated executions. The design separates a completed computation from authorization to release its result: required checks must pass, with recovery when they fail.
