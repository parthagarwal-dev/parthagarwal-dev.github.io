Explainable AI · Computer vision

Explainable gait abnormality detection

Conference paper · ICMLA 2025

Undergraduate research · Dr. Suman Saha
Penn State University · Nov 2024–Aug 2025

How can gait models use complementary movement representations while making their predictions easier to interpret?

Research problem

Gait analysis requires models that capture motion over time and provide interpretable evidence for their predictions. Our study considered two complementary representations: joint-based features from GAVD and silhouette sequences from OU-MVLP.

My contributions

I developed and trained CNN-LSTM models and built the PyTorch training pipeline. My work included sequence preprocessing, classical baselines such as nearest neighbors, decision trees, and random forests, and comparisons with deep-learning approaches.

I addressed class imbalance using SMOTE and loss weighting and evaluated on unseen identities. The paper incorporates SHAP for temporal attribution and Grad-CAM for spatial localization to examine what information the models use.

Publication

Parth Agarwal, Sangaa Chatterjee, Md Faisal Kabir, and Suman Saha. Explainable Gait Abnormality Detection Using Dual-Dataset CNN-LSTM Models. IEEE International Conference on Machine Learning and Applications (ICMLA), 2025.
